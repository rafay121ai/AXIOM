## 1. These terms

These terms are an agreement between you and [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME]
("Axiom", "we"). By signing in, you accept them. If you do not accept them,
do not use Axiom. The Privacy Policy explains what we do with your data.

## 2. What Axiom is

Axiom is an app for founders. You bring a real situation. Axiom reads what is
going on, teaches what you need from authors and from founders one stage
ahead, gives you one move to make today, and asks you that evening how it
went.

Axiom is free. There is no payment and nothing to buy. If we ever add paid
features, we will tell you first, and we will never charge you without your
clear agreement.

## 3. Who can use it

You must be [18] or older. [DECIDE] You sign in with Google and you are
responsible for your Google account. One account per person.

## 4. Your content

What you write in Axiom (situations, answers, conversations, reports) stays
yours.

You give us a licence to store, process and send it to our service providers
(including OpenAI) only to run Axiom for you. This licence ends when you
delete your account, except as below.

**If you choose to share a report**, you also give us a licence to make an
anonymised version of it and show it to other founders in Axiom. A person
reviews every story before anyone sees it. You can withdraw a share at any
time and the story is taken down. If you delete your account while a story is
published, the anonymised story stays and can no longer be linked to you or
taken down. Withdraw it first if you want it gone. The Privacy Policy has the
details.

You are responsible for what you write. Do not write other people's private
details that you have no right to share.

## 5. Acceptable use

Do not:
- break the law using Axiom;
- try to get into another person's account or data;
- attack, overload, scrape or reverse engineer the service;
- try to make Axiom produce harmful or illegal content;
- use Axiom to build a competing product from its output or its lessons;
- pretend to be someone else.

## 6. AI output is not advice

Axiom's Reads, lessons and moves are written with the help of AI. They can be
wrong, incomplete or out of date. They are not professional, financial,
investment, legal, tax, medical or mental health advice. Check anything that
matters with a qualified person. You decide what to do, and you are
responsible for what you do.

## 7. Care messages are not a crisis service

If Axiom sees signs that you may be in distress, it stops the journey and
points you to help. Axiom is not a counsellor, a doctor or an emergency
service, and it can miss signs or misread them. If you or someone else is in
danger, call your local emergency number now.

## 8. Author lessons and founder stories

Lessons quote and summarise published books. Every lesson names its author
and book. Axiom only shows passages checked against the source text. The books
belong to their authors and publishers. Axiom is not endorsed by them unless
it says so. [CHECK]

Founder stories are real outcomes shared with consent and reviewed by a
person. They describe what worked for one founder. They are not a promise of
what will work for you.

## 9. Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. Deletion happens 7 days
after you ask, and you can cancel during those 7 days. The Privacy Policy says
what is deleted.

Axiom never removes you, warns you or penalises you for missed moves, late
reports or a "Didn't". There are no strikes.

We may suspend or close an account only for serious breaches of section 5,
such as illegal use or attacks on the service. Where we can, we will tell you
why first. [DECIDE: see For Rafay, item 5]

We may update these terms. We will change the date at the top, and tell you in
