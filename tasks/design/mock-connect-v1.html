Connect

Where it shows

Once, right after the first reading, before Home.

One screen, one job. Tap Connect, Google (or Notion) opens in a sheet, you come back and the row turns to a gold tick. Not now skips it; it lives in Settings after that. The lines name no tool, so a new connector is just one more row.

Design notes

Rows are plain lines, no boxes. Icons are thin line drawings in writing colour (no brand logos, they would bring other colours in); the icon turns gold when connected. Connect is a text action with a gold hairline. Continue appears only once something is connected. Wash, no grid (this is not a journey screen). The switch is the app's existing one.
