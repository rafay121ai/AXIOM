Newsletters

When it shows

Once, right after Gmail connects

On the connect screen, when the founder comes back from Google with Gmail connected, Axiom spends a few seconds counting the newsletters in the last 30 days, then shows this one screen. Only if it found at least one. "Not now" skips it. After that it lives in Settings, Connections, Gmail, Newsletters.

How it works

Up to 8 newsletters, ranked by how often they arrive and how many the founder actually opens (Gmail's unread mark). Nothing is ticked for them: the founder picks. Only the ticked ones are read. When one says something that touches the founder's business, they get a signal about how it affects them, not a summary of the newsletter. The same story in two newsletters is one signal. Untick any time.
