The in-between states

Four moments that happen to every founder in the first week. All text is in Axiom's voice: plain, no "sorry", never a spinner. Tell me what to change in any of them.

1. Thinking

After the founder sends a message, the screen never freezes and never shows a spinner. A small logo draws itself in gold and glows, so the wait is nice to watch. Under it, one real quote that fits their situation, with the book's name. After about 4 seconds a small line says their message is saved. After about 10 seconds it becomes the error in section 2. Quotes come only from the verified library, word for word, and the server picks them to match the situation. If nothing fits, only the logo shows.

3. When there is no book to stand behind

Your rule: when there is no verified source, Axiom says nothing about it. It doesn't apologise, doesn't mention a missing book, and never makes one up. The lesson is built only from the founder's own facts, still has the picture, and still ends with a question. The server decides this, so the founder simply sees a shorter lesson.
