Your biggest customer is 40% of your revenue and wants 30% off at renewal. Run the number first: that discount takes 12% of everything you earn.

Chris Voss, in Never Split the Difference, says the strongest answer to a demand like this isn't a harder no. It's a question that asks for help. A "how" or "what" question gives the other side a sense of control, so they stop defending their number and start working on your problem. A flat no to a customer this big puts the whole renewal at risk.
