# Axiom user testing kit (Assignment 3, section B)

Goal: at least 10 founders who fit the target profile use the deployed app on their own and finish the journey through the Report. Personal or academic circle does not count toward the 10.

Target profile: building a company now, first-time founder, idea to pre-seed. Solo founders are a plus.

## 1. Where to find testers
- Founder communities: Indie Hackers, r/startups and r/Entrepreneur (check each one's self-promotion rules), startup Discord and Slack groups, LinkedIn posts.
- Your validation interviewees (ask them first: they already know the problem).
- Incubators and founder programs (ask the program manager to share it with their cohort).
- Ask every tester: "Who's one other founder who'd try this?"

Aim for 20 sign-ups to get 10 who finish: some will stop before the Report.

## 2. Outreach message (edit to your voice)
> Hi [name], I'm building Axiom, an app for founders who make hard calls alone. You bring a real situation, it tells you what's going on, teaches you from named books, and gives you one small move for today. Then it asks that evening how it went.
> I'm looking for 10 founders to try it for 15 minutes today and answer 2 minutes of questions after. Would you try it? [link]

## 3. What the tester does (send with the link)
1. Open [link] and sign in with Google.
2. Answer the short questions about you and your company.
3. Bring one real situation you're dealing with this week.
4. Go through the Read and Learn, and commit to the move.
5. This evening, open Axiom again and report how it went.
6. Fill in the 2-minute form: [form link].

Tell them: "Use it as you would alone. We won't help unless you're stuck." The rubric asks for independent use.

## 4. Feedback form (Tally or Google Forms)
1. What do you do, and what stage is your company? (text)
2. Which country are you in? (text)
3. How far did you get? (Onboarding / Read / Learn / Move / Report)
4. The Read described my situation accurately. (1–5)
5. The lesson was relevant to my situation. (1–5)
6. The move was clear and doable today. (1–5)
7. Did you do the move? (Done / Couldn't / Didn't / Haven't yet)
8. Where did you get stuck or confused? (text)
9. What was most useful? (text)
10. I would use Axiom for my next hard decision. (1–5)
11. Would you pay [$12] a month for it? (Yes / Maybe / No) and why? (text)
12. Can we quote you anonymously in our report? (Yes / No)

## 5. Observation sheet (for 2–3 testers you watch on a call)
Ask them to share their screen and think out loud. Don't help.

## 6. Consent line (top of the form)
"Your answers are used for a university assignment and to improve Axiom. We won't share your name. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time from Settings."

## 7. Tracking
After each tester, fill one row in report.md section B1. On the evening of each test day, check who reported and send one reminder message.
