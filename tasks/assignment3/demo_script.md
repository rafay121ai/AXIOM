# Demo video script (4:30 target, 5:00 max)

Rule from the assignment: show the product working, not slides. One title card at the start, then screen recording only.
Record on the live server with a real situation. Cut AI waiting time in editing, but keep one short wait so it is clear the AI is real.

| Time | Show on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | Title card: "Axiom. For founders who decide alone." | "A third of new startups now have one founder. When they hit a hard call, there's usually no one to ask. Axiom is for that moment: a founder brings a real situation and leaves with one move for today." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Home screen, then the journey labels | "The journey is Situation, Read, Learn, Move, Report. Here's one founder going all the way through." |
| 0:40–1:10 | Sign-in, a few onboarding taps, the first reading | "Onboarding is 13 taps. Then the first AI step: Axiom describes the founder back to them, using only words they gave it. The server checks every quote is theirs." |
| 1:10–1:50 | Typing the situation, 1–2 follow-up questions, "Got it. Here's what I see." | "The founder types what's going on in their own words. Axiom asks at most two questions. That limit is enforced in code, not left to the AI." |
| 1:50–2:30 | The Read appearing | "The Read. It names what's really going on, and the underlined phrases are the founder's own words. This uses our strongest model, because getting this right is what earns trust." |
| 2:30–3:20 | Learn: a lesson, the author and book shown, a tapped follow-up, the move card | "Learn finds the book passages that fit this situation and teaches from them. Every lesson names the author and the book, and quotes are checked word for word against the source. No invented quotes. After two real replies, Axiom offers the move." |
| 3:20–3:50 | The Move sheet, committing it, the report time | "One move for today. Our validator checks the founder is the only one who has to act, it starts in ten minutes, and it either happens or it doesn't. Committing schedules the evening report." |
| 3:50–4:15 | The Report: Done / Couldn't / Didn't, the share choice | "That evening: Done, Couldn't or Didn't. No guilt, no streaks. The founder chooses whether to share the outcome anonymously, and a person reviews it before anyone sees it." |
| 4:15–4:30 | Back on Home | "In fifteen minutes the founder went from a stuck decision to a clear read, a cited lesson, and something they did today. Every report also builds what no general AI has: what founders actually did, and how it went." |

Recording tips:
- Wake the server first (open the app 2 minutes before recording).
- For the Report, commit a move at least an hour before recording (reports open after that).
- Use a phone-sized window, large text, no notifications on screen.
- Don't show the care screen with real distress words; describe it in the report instead.
